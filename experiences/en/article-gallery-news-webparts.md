# CNAV StellaiR: Regional Article Gallery and News WebParts

**Role:** Developer
**Solutions:** SharePoint, SPFx, PnP PowerShell

## Description

Made CNAV's central articles easy to reach for its 20+ local branches.
- Built a news WebPart that brings each branch its region's articles on its own site, with compact and detailed modes.
- Made gallery and featured-article WebParts configurable per page, reusable for any document type, with a latest-article fallback.
- Scripted the provisioning of home-page WebParts and two gallery pages (branch-only and all articles), removing manual page setup.
